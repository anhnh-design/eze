Support

Frequently asked questions

Which iPhones can use EzE?

Any iPhone on iOS 18 or later. The Action Button is on iPhone 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, the iPhone 16 lineup, 16e, the iPhone 17 lineup and iPhone Air. Other models use Back Tap, Control Center or the Lock Screen.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. The app has no account and no server holding your transactions. See the Privacy Policy.

Will I lose my data if I delete and reinstall the app?

Yes. The app has no iCloud backup of its own. Before deleting the app or switching phones, go to Settings › Export & Import to save an Excel or CSV file, then import it on the new device.

The Action Button screenshot doesn't work?

In the Shortcuts app, check that the EzE shortcut is assigned to the Action Button (iOS Settings › Action Button) and that you chose “Always Allow” when the shortcut asked for permission. You can replay the setup guide from Settings › Setup guide in the app.

Bought EzE Pro and switched phones?

Open the EzE Pro screen and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID you bought with.
